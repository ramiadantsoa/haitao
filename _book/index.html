Preface

This book should not exist. There are already so many free available materials online. Many universities have materials and open courses (examples). There are PDF and books available. There are videos on YouTube, some might be more legitimate than others. I also hope that there are libraries where you are and you have access to recent up-to-date physical books (e.g. at MBC)…if you are student do not hesitate to make a request to your advisors and acquaintances, still stay reasonable. As far as know, foreign researchers are not shy on bringing knowledge. And ultimately, I expect that the major part of what is written here are taught during undergraduate and graduate courses at your institutions.

So is there a need for this book? Well, I aim and try to address superficial problems with this book. The first problem is decentralized information. Maybe there is a book on coding and another book on statistics. Maybe in some books, the basics are not covered so you need to look somewhere else. Maybe the levels differ. The first aim to gather in one place multiple informations, maybe this is not a book but an encyclopedia. This cover topics that I feel are not understood (essentially basics) or most commonly used without reflection (say PCA or ANOVA). Starts with a basic level, maybe at most intermediate level. I expect it to grow and be updated overtime although I cannot guarantee the regularity of the update. Hopefully, this will also include more advanced topics in the future. Although as above, if you look for advanced, maybe this is not the book for you.

The second is language, maybe we are not all comfortable with English. Although with mixed opinions, this book will be in Malagasy (an English version is also available although less polished). Unfortunately, writing in Malagasy is a huge challenge. I had to make a decision for the translation. First, I used termed defined by Malagasy Academy. Second, if the former does not exist, I aimed to translate the idea. Finally, if both fails, I would Malagasize the word and provide a definition. See glossary at the end. But I hope that breaking the language barrier will make it to more accessible.

Third, this book/encyclopedia is written in a compact form to combine the intuitive, the formal, and applied perspectives. Each topic, will be divided into 5 parts. The first part will provide the intuition for the concept, I will use metaphor etc. The second part will focus on the formal definition which could be Googled. The third part will provide a practical side, how to run those things in R. The fourth part will provide tips etc. The last part is on documentation if one wants to go further.

As a warning, it is worth mentioning that I am no expert in any of the topics and sections. I am mostly self-taught training in statistics and programming. There are experts in the field, and I will never dare to compare to them. This book is based on my own experience and the way how I understand the concept, the way how I code. This is more a generalist approach to the concept. Yet, to enhance the value, I had colleagues and past advisors–the experts–that made sure that what is written is not completely nonsense. I am grateful and forever in dept to Otso, Tony, Guillaume, Gavin, Eva. However, if there is something wrong, I take full responsibility.

This is free book, you can download it from my webpage and MBC page Feel free to share it with anyone, use on whatever you want but this is self-edited book so you should not use it as a valid citation. As the proverb says, Saonjo iray lohasa. Someone might try to make a business out of it. I am sorry for those who will pay for it and get scammed.

Finally, don’t hesitate to reach out if there is something wrong, you will help me greatly.
